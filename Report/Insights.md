## 1. Which game genres dominate the Steam marketplace?

### Visualization

![Top Genres](../Images/Genre%20Analysis/01_top_genres.png)

### Key Findings

- **Indie** is by far the most common genre on Steam, with over **82,000** titles.
- **Casual**, **Action**, and **Adventure** are also highly represented, each containing tens of thousands of games.
- Genres such as **Sports**, **Racing**, **Utilities**, and **Animation & Modelling** occupy only a small fraction of the marketplace.
- The large number of Indie, Action, Adventure, and Casual games indicates that these genres are highly competitive for new developers.

### Business Insight

Developers entering popular genres should focus on creating a unique selling point, as competition is significantly higher. Smaller genres may offer lower competition but could also serve a smaller audience.

## 2. Which genres receive the highest average user ratings?

### Visualization

![Highest Rated Genres](../Images/Genre%20Analysis/02_highest_rated_genres.png)

### Key Findings

- **Casual**, **Indie**, **Free To Play**, **Adventure**, and **Action** achieve the highest average review scores, all averaging approximately **75–77% positive reviews**.
- The average review scores among the top genres differ by only a few percentage points, suggesting that no single genre consistently outperforms the others in player satisfaction.
- Several niche categories such as **Photo Editing**, **Game Development**, **Design & Illustration**, and **Audio Production** also maintain strong average ratings despite having relatively few titles.

### Business Insight

Genre alone does not appear to determine player satisfaction. Well-designed games can achieve strong review scores across many genres, indicating that execution and game quality are likely more important than genre selection.
